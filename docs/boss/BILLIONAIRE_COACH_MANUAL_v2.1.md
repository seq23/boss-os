# **PART VII — PILLARS, PROJECTS & ARBITRATION**

*How to Manage Competing Priorities*

*\>"You can do anything, but you cannot do everything at once. The system forces you to choose, so reality doesn't choose for you."*

## **1\. PILLARS VS. PROJECTS**

Before you can arbitrate, you must understand the difference between a Pillar and a Project.

• Pillars are permanent. They are the core domains of your life that must not collapse (e.g., Health, Wealth, Family, Mind). You never "finish" a pillar; you just maintain it. If you try to aggressively advance all your pillars at the same time, you will burn out.  
• Projects are temporary. They are specific initiatives with an end date (e.g., Launch V2, Lose 10 lbs). Projects live inside of Pillars.

## **2\. THE FOREGROUND PRIORITY (The Secret Sauce)**

This is where amateur planners fail. If you ask an AI for a schedule, it will give you a generic, equal list of tasks across all your pillars (e.g., 1 hour of sales, 1 hour of gym, 1 hour of writing). Billionaires do not work like that. They choose ONE Foreground Priority.

• The Foreground: Exactly ONE project lives here. It gets your aggressive, creative, problem-solving energy.  
• The Background: Everything else lives here. You do just enough maintenance to keep these pillars from collapsing, but you do not try to advance them.

## **3\. THE ARBITRATION ENGINE**

If you have more than one active project, the system must initiate Arbitration. Do not use your gut feeling. Run conflicting priorities through this filter. The winner takes the Foreground.

| Factor | The Question to Ask |
| :---- | :---- |
| Leverage | Does this create disproportionate value relative to the effort? (1hr work \= 100hr output) |
| Urgency | Is there a real external deadline or compounding cost of delay? (Fake urgency scores 0). |
| Energy Match | Does my current biological state match the demands of this task? |
| Compounding | Does this build an asset that pays me back later, or is it just labor? |
| Downside | If I ignore this today, is the risk survivable? |

## **4\. THE 30-60-90 DAY FRAMEWORK**

We do not build 1-year plans. They are too fragile. When you run Prompt \#3 (Build Execution Plan), the system builds a 90-day roadmap broken into three strict gates:

• 30-Day Gate (Foundation): Prove the concept.  
• 60-Day Gate (Build): Create the leverage.  
• 90-Day Gate (Establish): Lock the asset.

Promotion / Parking Gates: At the end of 90 days, you face a forced decision: Promote, Maintain, or Park the project. Parking a project is a successful outcome—it means you made a strategic allocation choice.
