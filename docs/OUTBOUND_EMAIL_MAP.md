# Outbound Email Map

Last verified 12 Oct 2026. Memphis Senior Care Homes rows added 13 Oct 2026.

Question answered: "What controls all the emails going out from The Industry Guides and all side businesses? Is it a Mac launchd job only?"

Scope: Boss OS and the side businesses it runs, only. The fund's sending paths are not listed here; they live in the fund's own repo and never share a repo, a seat or a ledger with Boss OS.

## Answer

- **No. Of 21 live sending paths, 6 run from launchd on the Mac, 8 run in Cloudflare (4 on Worker crons, 4 on a form or purchase), 6 run on GitHub Actions schedules and 1 happens only inside a Claude session. 2 more look like senders but are switched off.**
- Mac-dependent (6): every Boss OS push email to you (task notices, repo-change results, YouTube comment watch, the KDP surface report and vault intake confirmations, from <name>@sequoiataylor.com via Resend, falling back to Boss OS <hello@theindustryguides.com> only if that sender is refused) and the CI-sweep issues. All of them go to you, never to outsiders. A sleeping Mac delays them; it never sends anything late to a stranger.
- Cloud, Mac off or on (14): everything that reaches people outside: Monique's outreach for the Industry Guides and four side businesses (Boss OS Worker, hourly, Gmail API as st@time-2-read.com), the Industry Guides lead forms (the five canonicals and the hub's function), ApprovalPrep and wedding-checklist purchase email, Creator Studios, A Player Mode, client properties, plus the Industry Guides' weekly search-outcome report and the How We Know and backlink-network issues on GitHub Actions.
- Not yet sending (6, added 13 Oct 2026): Memphis Senior Care Homes, a property of The Industry Guides. Monique's outreach for it (Boss OS Worker, hourly, from hello@theindustryguides.com like every guide, waiting on that route) and five site emails sent by its own Pages Functions as The Industry Guides through Resend (lead notification to you, teasers, releases and pass notices to communities, 30/60/90-day family follow-ups). All cloud; none reaches anyone until its sender is live.
- Session-only (1): the claude.ai Gmail connector, which sends as seq.taylor@gmail.com only while a Claude session (or a session cron) is open.

## Method

- launchd: `launchctl list`, every plist in ~/Library/LaunchAgents and ~/bin/launchd (program and schedule); no crontab exists.
- Code: `git grep` on origin/main of every repo under ~/GitHub (plus ~/aplayer-mode, ~/bin) for Resend, Gmail send, SMTP, MailChannels, SendGrid, Postmark, Mailgun, nodemailer, send_email; workflows grepped for mail and issue creation.
- Cloudflare: wrangler.toml crons and email vars per repo; Pages secret names (not values) on two guides projects.
- Not read: the local-guides-generator senior-* functions (not on main on 13 Oct 2026), live D1 switch state for Monique's outreach, Pages dashboard variables for the five guides lead forms, GitHub watch settings.
