## Answer

- **No.** Emails run from four kinds of place: the cloud on a schedule (Cloudflare Worker crons and GitHub Actions, including Boss OS jobs its own five-minute clock has dispatched since 21 Nov 2026), the cloud when something happens (a form, a purchase, a tap), the Mac (launchd jobs, which wait while it sleeps), and a Claude session. Every Boss OS email to you goes through the plain layout and the send-time gate (`postEmployeeMail` → `mailGate`, `scripts/ops/notify.mjs`); most other producers write a section of Simone's one morning email instead of sending.
- **The counts are on the page, never here** (8 Nov 2026: this section said "31 live sending paths, 9 on launchd" while the manifest held 93 rows, and Simone's morning email had moved off the Mac a week earlier). The Email Map page counts the manifest every time it renders.

Not senders: Cloudflare Email Routing is inbound only: hello@theindustryguides.com → industry-guides-mail-fanout Worker → the Industry Guides inbox (Gmail) and st@time-2-read.com (13 Oct 2026; Email Routing allows one destination per rule, so a tiny Worker in boss-os, src/mail-fanout/index.ts, forwards to both, and the outreach engine sees the guides' replies in st@'s mailbox); Buffer is social.
